# Historical records — not current instructions

Week 2 dataset, QA, model-runtime and completion records, with their frozen manifests.

Nothing in this directory is an active requirement. Statements such as "HOLD",
"pending clearance", "requires approval" or "zero admitted files" describe the
day they were written. Current scope, authority and status are in
[docs/project](../project/STATUS.md); [HISTORY.md](../project/HISTORY.md)
explains what each group of documents was for.

The files are kept unedited because several are bound by recorded checksums and
because they are the provenance of results that still stand. Do not change them
to make new work pass; put new policy, runs and results in new files.
